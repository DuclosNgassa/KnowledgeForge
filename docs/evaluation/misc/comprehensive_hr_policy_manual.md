# CHAPTER 2: EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATIONS & WORKPLACE STANDARDS

### 2.1. Employment Categories

To clarify eligibility for benefits, overtime, and leave accruals, Aetheria Technologies classifies employees according
to the following definitions:

* **Regular Full-Time Employee:** An employee hired to work an ongoing schedule of at least 40 hours per week. Regular
  full-time employees are eligible for the complete range of company benefits, subject to policy terms.
* **Regular Part-Time Employee:** An employee hired to work an ongoing schedule of fewer than 30 hours per week. Regular
  part-time employees receive prorated PTO and statutory benefits, but may have limited eligibility for certain health
  insurance or equity programs.
* **Temporary / Fixed-Term Employee:** An employee hired for a specific project, seasonal workload, or defined calendar
  duration (not to exceed 12 consecutive months). Temporary employees receive statutory benefits only, unless otherwise
  stipulated in writing.
* **Independent Contractors / Consultants:** External service providers engaged under a Statement of Work (SOW) or
  Master Services Agreement (MSA). Contractors are **not** employees of Aetheria, are ineligible for employee benefits,
  and must not represent themselves as internal staff.

---

### 2.2. FLSA Exemption Status

In accordance with the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable local labor standards, every job position at
Aetheria is evaluated and designated as either Exempt or Non-Exempt:

| Exemption Class | Qualification Criteria                                                                                          | Overtime Eligibility                                   | Time Tracking                               |
|:----------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------|
| **Exempt**      | Executive, administrative, professional, or specialized computer roles meeting legal salary threshold tests.    | Ineligible for overtime pay; paid a set salary.        | Exception-based tracking (PTO, leave only). |
| **Non-Exempt**  | Roles that do not meet statutory salary or duties tests (e.g., support desk tier 1, administrative assistants). | Eligible for overtime (1.5x base rate over 40 hrs/wk). | Must log all actual hours worked daily.     |

---

### 2.3. Introductory / Probationary Period

All newly hired regular employees undergo a **90-calendar-day Introductory Period** starting on their official date of
hire.

* **Purpose:** The introductory period allows the employee and their manager to assess job fit, technical competence,
  performance metrics, and alignment with company culture.
* **Evaluation:** At the 45-day and 85-day marks, the manager will conduct structured check-ins. Successful completion
  of the 90-day period will be confirmed in writing by Human Resources.
* **Extension:** Aetheria reserves the right to extend the introductory period for up to an additional 30 to 60 days if
  performance or attendance needs continued evaluation.
* **At-Will Nature:** Completion of the introductory period does not alter the at-will status of employment nor
  guarantee continued employment for any fixed duration.

### 2.6. Anti-Harassment, Anti-Discrimination, and Anti-Retaliation

Aetheria Technologies maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of harassment, discrimination, or
retaliation in the workplace.

#### Definition of Sexual Harassment:

Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal, visual, or physical conduct of a sexual nature
constitute sexual harassment when:

1. Submission to such conduct is made an explicit or implicit term or condition of employment;
2. Submission to or rejection of such conduct is used as the basis for employment decisions;
3. Such conduct creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.

#### Complaint & Investigation Procedure:

```
Step 1: Incident Occurs -> Report to HRBP, Manager, or Anonymous Ethics Hotline
Step 2: Intake & Assessment -> HR initiates formal inquiry within 24 hours
Step 3: Investigation -> Neutral interviews & evidence collection
Step 4: Resolution & Action -> Disciplinary steps taken if policy violation found
Step 5: Follow-up -> Check-in with reporting party to ensure no retaliation
```

* **Reporting Channels:** Employees can report concerns directly to their manager, any HR Business Partner, or
  anonymously via the **Aetheria Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-ETHX)** or ethics web portal.
* **Retaliation Prohibited:** Aetheria strictly prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports harassment or
  discrimination in good faith, or who participates in an investigation. Anyone found engaging in retaliation will face
  immediate termination of employment.

# CHAPTER 3: HOURS OF WORK, ATTENDANCE, AND REMOTE WORK

### 3.1. Standard Work Schedules & Core Operating Hours

Aetheria Technologies operates across multiple global time zones. Standard operational guidelines are designed to
maintain team alignment while supporting work-life flexibility.

```
Standard Workweek: 40 hours per week (Monday through Friday)
Core Collaboration Hours: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Local Regional Time)
Flexible Windows: 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM (Start) | 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM (End)
```

During **Core Operating Hours**, employees are expected to be online, accessible via company communication channels (
Slack, email), and available for scheduled meetings unless taking an approved break or leave.

---

### 3.2. Timekeeping & Attendance Records

Accurate timekeeping is a legal requirement and a key operational standard.

* **Non-Exempt Employees:** Non-exempt personnel must record all actual working hours using the company HRIS
  time-tracking system. This includes logging start times, meal breaks, and end-of-day clock-outs. Working "
  off-the-clock" is strictly forbidden.
* **Exempt Employees:** Exempt employees do not log daily hours for payroll calculation, but must submit requests for
  PTO, sick leave, and holidays through the HRIS for record-keeping.
* **Tardiness & Absenteeism:** Regular attendance is essential. Employees unable to report to work on time must notify
  their direct manager via Slack or email at least 30 minutes prior to their scheduled start time. Unexcused absences
  for three (3) consecutive working days without communication will be treated as voluntary job abandonment (
  resignation).

---

### 3.3. Meal and Rest Breaks

Non-exempt employees receive mandatory rest and meal periods based on working shift lengths:

* **Rest Breaks:** One paid 15-minute rest period for every 4 hours worked. Rest breaks should be taken near the middle
  of each 4-hour work block.
* **Meal Breaks:** Employees working shifts longer than 5 hours are required to take an unpaid, uninterrupted meal break
  of at least 30 to 60 minutes. Employees must fully disengage from all work responsibilities during unpaid meal breaks.

---

### 3.4. Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

Aetheria Technologies supports a dynamic hybrid workplace model structured around three primary work arrangements:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                      WORK LOCATION DESIGNATIONS                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Office-Centric  | 4-5 days/week in corporate office              |
| 2. Hybrid          | 2-3 days/week office | 2-3 days/week remote   |
| 3. Fully Remote    | 100% remote working from approved location    |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### Remote Work Expectations:

1. **Approved Jurisdictions:** Remote employees must work from their registered, HR-approved primary residential
   address. Due to tax, legal, and regulatory constraints, working from a different state or country for more than 14
   cumulative days in a calendar year requires prior written approval from HR and Finance.
2. **Dedicated Workspace:** Remote staff must maintain an environment free from significant noise and distractions,
   equipped with high-speed internet (minimum 50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload).
3. **Data Security:** Remote workers must adhere strictly to corporate IT security protocols, including using corporate
   Virtual Private Networks (VPN), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and secure Wi-Fi networks. Public Wi-Fi without
   VPN is strictly prohibited.

# CHAPTER 4: COMPENSATION, SALARY STRUCTURE, AND PAYROLL

### 4.1. Total Rewards Philosophy

Aetheria Technologies strives to maintain a competitive Total Rewards structure that attracts, retains, and motivates
exceptional talent globally.

Our compensation framework combines:

* **Base Salary:** Market-competitive fixed pay reflective of job scope, skill requirements, and regional cost-of-labor
  data.
* **Variable Incentive Pay:** Performance-based annual bonuses or sales commissions aligned with individual metrics and
  organizational targets.
* **Long-Term Equity Incentives:** Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) or Stock Options for eligible bands to align employee
  interests with long-term company value creation.
* **Comprehensive Benefits:** Premium healthcare coverage, retirement matching, and holistic wellness stipends.

---

### 4.2. Salary Bands, Job Architecture, and Benchmarking

Every position at Aetheria is assigned a specific Job Level and Salary Band within our Job Architecture Framework:

```
LEVEL      TITLE BAND                   ENGINEERING / PRODUCT        CORPORATE / HR / FINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L1         Associate / Entry Level      Associate Engineer           HR Coordinator
L2         Intermediate Specialist      Software Engineer I          HR Generalist
L3         Senior Professional          Senior Software Engineer     HR Business Partner
L4         Lead / Staff Specialist      Staff Engineer / Tech Lead   Senior HR Business Partner
L5         Principal / Manager          Principal Engineer / Mgr     HR Director
L6         Director / Fellow            Distinguished Engineer       Senior Director
L7+        Executive Leadership         VP / SVP / C-Suite           VP / SVP / C-Suite
```

* **Market Benchmarking:** Salary bands are benchmarked annually against industry peer groups using Radford and Mercer
  compensation datasets. Bands are adjusted for geographic cost-of-labor differentials (e.g., Tier 1 Tech Hubs vs.
  National Average).
* **Pay Transparency:** Employees may view the salary band assigned to their specific job level and geographic tier by
  accessing the internal HR portal.

---

### 4.3. Payroll Processing & Pay Schedule

Payroll is processed systematically across all operational entities:

* **Pay Frequency:**
    * **United States & Canada:** Bi-weekly (26 pay periods per year), with paydays falling on alternating Fridays.
    * **EMEA & APAC:** Monthly, paid on the 25th of each calendar month (or the preceding business day if the 25th falls
      on a weekend or holiday).
* **Direct Deposit:** Direct deposit into the employee's designated bank account is the mandatory payment method, unless
  prohibited by local statutes. Employees can update banking information securely via the HRIS portal.

---

### 4.4. Overtime Compensation & Authorization

Overtime work is strictly managed to ensure non-exempt employees are fairly compensated and workload expectations remain
sustainable.

```
Daily / Weekly Threshold          Overtime Rate
-------------------------------------------------------------------
> 40 hours in a single workweek   1.5x Base Hourly Rate
> 8 hours in a single workday     1.5x Base Hourly Rate (where required by state law)
> 12 hours in a single workday    2.0x Base Hourly Rate (where required by state law)
7th Consecutive Day of Work       1.5x for first 8 hrs; 2.0x thereafter
```

* **Prior Authorization Required:** Non-exempt employees must obtain written approval from their manager *before*
  working overtime hours. Working unauthorized overtime is a policy violation subject to disciplinary action, though all
  hours worked will be paid as required by law.

---

### 4.5. Performance-Based Bonuses & Variable Compensation

Many roles at Aetheria offer eligibility for annual discretionary performance bonuses or monthly/quarterly sales
commission structures.

* **Corporate Bonus Plan (CBP):**
    * **Target Multiplier:** CBP targets range from 5% to 30% of base salary depending on job level (L1–L7+).
    * **Performance Weighting:** Bonus payouts are calculated using a weighted formula:

      $$\text{Total Bonus Payout} = \text{Base Salary} \times \text{Target \%} \times \left( 0.6 \times \text{Company Goal Attainment} + 0.4 \times \text{Individual Performance Rating} \right)$$

    * **Eligibility Date:** To receive an annual bonus payout, employees must be actively employed by Aetheria on the
      date bonuses are paid, and must have been hired on or before October 1st of the performance year.

---

### 4.6. Equity Grants & Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

To foster a culture of shared ownership, eligible full-time employees may receive long-term equity grants under the
Aetheria Equity Incentive Plan.

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                   STANDARD EQUITY VESTING SCHEDULE                 |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Grant Date       : Initial Grant Approved by Board of Directors    |
| Year 1 (Cliff)   : 25% of total grant vests on 12-month anniversary  |
| Years 2 to 4     : Remaining 75% vests in equal monthly increments |
| Total Duration   : 48-Month Full Vesting Period                    |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

* **Refresh Grants:** High-performing employees (L3 and above) may be considered for annual equity refresh grants during
  annual performance review cycles.
* **Plan Governance:** All equity grants are subject to approval by the Board of Directors Compensation Committee and
  are governed by the terms of individual Equity Grant Agreements.

---

### 4.7. Payroll Deductions & Garnishments

Statutory and voluntary payroll deductions are itemized on each pay stub:

1. **Mandatory Statutory Deductions:** Federal, state, and local income taxes, social security, Medicare, state
   disability, and statutory unemployment insurances.
2. **Voluntary Deductions:** Health/dental premiums, retirement contributions (401k/RRSP), HSA/FSA contributions, and
   employee stock purchase plan contributions.
3. **Garnishments:** Court-ordered child support, tax levies, or wage garnishments will be processed strictly in
   accordance with legal mandates. HR will notify affected employees in writing upon receipt of official garnishment
   notices.

---

### 4.8. Correction of Pay Errors

Aetheria makes every effort to ensure compensation is accurate. If an error occurs on a paycheck:

* **Reporting:** The employee must immediately submit a payroll inquiry ticket via the HR Helpdesk.
* **Resolution:** Underpayments will be corrected via an administrative off-cycle payment within **two (2) business days
  ** of verification. Overpayments will be recovered through a mutually agreed-upon payroll deduction schedule in
  compliance with local wage payment laws.


#### The 5-Point Performance Rating Scale:

* **Rating 5 (Exceptional Impact):** Consistently exceeds all expectations; drives game-changing organizational
  outcomes.
* **Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations):** Frequently goes beyond job requirements; delivers high-quality work reliably.
* **Rating 3 (Meets Expectations):** Consistently delivers target results; solid, reliable performer across key
  responsibilities.
* **Rating 2 (Needs Improvement):** Inconsistently meets expectations; immediate developmental guidance required.
* **Rating 1 (Unsatisfactory):** Consistently fails to meet basic requirements; performance improvement plan mandatory.

---

### 5.2. Continuous Feedback & 1-on-1 Framework

Annual reviews are designed to summarize ongoing, real-time feedback—not present unexpected information.

* **1-on-1 Meetings:** Direct managers must conduct mandatory **weekly or bi-weekly 1-on-1 meetings** (minimum 30
  minutes) with each direct report to review progress, clear blockers, and discuss career goals.
* **Quarterly OKR Reviews:** Individual and team Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) are set quarterly, published
  transparently in the company goal platform, and reviewed every 90 days.


* **Effective Date:** Approved merit increases take effect on April 1st of the following calendar year.
* **Budget Limits:** Merit budgets are finalized annually by the Finance & HR Compensation Committee based on company
  profitability and macroeconomic benchmarks.

# CHAPTER 6: TIME OFF, VACATION, AND PAID LEAVE

### 6.1. Paid Time Off (PTO) / Vacation Accrual Policy

Aetheria believes that rest and rejuvenation are essential for sustained creativity, productivity, and personal
well-being. Regular full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) every pay period starting on their first day of work.

```
Tenure Tier               Annual PTO Days   Accrual Rate per Bi-Weekly Pay Period
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0 to 2 Years Service      15 Days (120 hrs) 4.615 Hours
3 to 5 Years Service      20 Days (160 hrs) 6.153 Hours
6+ Years Service          25 Days (200 hrs) 7.692 Hours
```

* **Proration:** Employees hired mid-year accrue PTO on a prorated basis relative to their start date.
* **Part-time Accrual:** Regular part-time employees working 20+ hours accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis proportional to
  standard 40-hour schedules.

### 6.2. PTO Scheduling, Requests, and Manager Approval

* **Advance Request Notice:** Requests for planned PTO of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted via the HRIS at

  order received, with conflict priority resolved by tenure where necessary.
* **Peak Seasons:** Departments with critical coverage requirements (e.g., Customer Support during platform updates,
  Finance at year-end) may establish specific blackout dates for PTO with 60 days advance notice.

* **Cash-Out Policy:** Unused PTO cannot be cashed out during active employment except where mandated by local law. Upon

# CHAPTER 7: SICK LEAVE, MEDICAL, AND FAMILY LEAVE

### 7.1. Paid Sick Leave Accrual and Permitted Uses

Aetheria provides dedicated Paid Sick Leave to support employee recovery and health care needs without financial
disruption.

* **Sick Leave Entitlement:** Full-time regular employees receive **10 fully paid sick days** (80 hours) frontloaded on
  January 1st of each calendar year.
* **Permitted Uses:** Paid sick leave may be used for:
    1. Diagnosis, care, or treatment of the employee's own physical or mental illness, injury, or medical condition.
    2. Preventive medical, dental, or vision care appointments for the employee.
    3. Caring for an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or legal dependent) requiring
       care or medical appointment assistance.
    4. Seeking legal or medical assistance related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking.

---

### 7.2. Medical Call-in Procedures & Doctor Certification

* **Notification Protocol:** Employees unable to report to work due to sudden illness must notify their manager via
  Slack or email no later than **30 minutes prior** to their scheduled start time.
* **Medical Certification:** For sick leave absences lasting **three (3) or more consecutive working days**, Aetheria
  reserves the right to request a medical note from a licensed healthcare provider confirming fitness to return to work.
  Specific diagnostic details are not required to maintain employee medical privacy.

---

### 7.3. Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) & Statutory Leaves

In accordance with federal laws (and equivalent local statutory mandates), eligible employees are entitled to
job-protected leave for qualifying family and medical reasons.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        FMLA ELIGIBILITY & TERMS                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Service Requirement : At least 12 months of service at Aetheria  |
| Hours Worked        : At least 1,250 hours in preceding 12 months |
| Entitlement         : Up to 12 workweeks of unpaid, job-protected |
|                       leave within a rolling 12-month period.     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### Qualifying FMLA Reasons:

* The birth of a child, or placement of a child with the employee for adoption or foster care.
* To care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition.
* A serious health condition that renders the employee unable to perform their job duties.
* Qualifying exigencies arising from a family member’s active military deployment.

*Benefit Continuation:* Aetheria maintains health insurance benefits for employees on approved FMLA leave under the same
conditions as if the employee had continued working.

### 7.5. Short-Term and Long-Term Disability Insurance

Aetheria provides fully company-paid Short-Term Disability (STD) and Long-Term Disability (LTD) coverage for all regular

* **Short-Term Disability (STD):**
    * **Coverage:** Begins on the 8th consecutive calendar day of medically certified disability.
    * **Benefit:** Replaces **66.67% of base weekly earnings** up to a maximum of $2,500/week for up to 26 weeks.
* **Long-Term Disability (LTD):**
    * **Coverage:** Takes effect after 180 consecutive days of disability.
    * **Benefit:** Replaces **60% of base monthly salary** up to a maximum of $10,000/month, continuing until age 65 or
      recovery.

# CHAPTER 10: SEPARATION OF EMPLOYMENT & EXIT PROCEDURES

### 10.1. Voluntary Resignation & Notice Periods

To ensure smooth business continuity and handover of responsibilities, employees choosing to resign are asked to provide
written notice:

```
Job Level Tier                             Required Notice Period
------------------------------------------------------------------
Individual Contributors (L1 - L3)          2 Weeks (10 Business Days)
Managers & Lead Engineers (L4 - L5)        4 Weeks (20 Business Days)
Directors & Executives (L6+)               60 Calendar Days
```

* **Notice Period Working Expectations:** Resigning employees are expected to spend their notice period actively
  assisting with project transition, documentation, and handover training. Aetheria reserves the right to pay out the
  notice period in lieu of active work at management discretion.

---

### 10.2. Involuntary Termination & Layoffs

Involuntary separation occurs when employment is ended by the company due to performance, conduct, structural
reorganization, or economic conditions.

* **Termination for Cause:** Immediate termination without advance notice or severance for severe policy violations,
  fraud, gross misconduct, or illegal acts.
* **Reduction in Force (RIF) / Layoffs:** In cases of operational restructuring, affected employees will receive advance
  written notice in compliance with the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act or local statutory
  notice rules, along with severance packages evaluated based on tenure.

---

### 10.3. Retirement

Employees planning to retire are encouraged to provide written notification to HR at least **ninety (90) days in advance
** to facilitate pension/401k distribution options and succession planning.

---

### 10.4. Return of Company Property

On or before their last day of active employment, departing employees must return all corporate assets to the IT
Offboarding Team or designated hub manager.

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     COMPANY ASSETS RETURN CHECKLIST                |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [ ] Laptop, chargers, and external hardware peripherals             |
| [ ] Security YubiKeys, smart cards, and physical office keycards   |

| [ ] Company-issued mobile devices or monitors                       |
| [ ] Physical files, documents, and corporate records               |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

* **Failure to Return Property:** Failure to return corporate hardware or deliberate deletion/destruction of company
  data will result in legal action and potential withholding of final pay where permitted by local laws.

---

### 10.5. Final Paycheck & Offboarding Logistics

* **Final Paycheck Timing:** Final wages, including earned base pay, unused accrued PTO balances, and statutory
  adjustments, will be paid on the next scheduled payday or earlier as required by local state laws (e.g., same day for
  involuntary terminations in California).
* **Benefits Termination & COBRA:** Employee health insurance coverage ends on the last calendar day of the month in
  which separation occurs. Eligible US employees will receive information regarding COBRA continuing coverage options
  via mail from the third-party administrator within 14 days of separation.

---

### 10.6. Exit Interviews

HR conducts voluntary, confidential Exit Interviews with all resigning regular employees prior to their departure.

* **Objectives:** To gather feedback regarding corporate culture, management effectiveness, compensation fairness, and
  workplace environment improvements.
* **Aggregate Reporting:** Feedback from exit interviews is anonymized and aggregated to inform annual HR initiatives
  and executive strategy.

---

### 10.7. Post-Employment Obligations (NDA & Non-Solicitation)

Separation from employment does not relieve departing staff of ongoing legal obligations:

1. **Nondisclosure Agreement (NDA):** Confidentiality obligations regarding Aetheria’s trade secrets, software source
   code, customer databases, and proprietary metrics remain binding indefinitely post-employment.
2. **Non-Solicitation:** For a period of **twelve (12) months** following separation, former employees may not directly
   or indirectly solicit, induce, or encourage any active Aetheria employee to leave their employment to join a
   competing entity.
3. **Reference Checks:** Aetheria follows a standard neutral reference policy. External employment verification requests
   will only confirm job title, dates of employment, and salary history (upon written employee release).
